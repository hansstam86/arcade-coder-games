📈 Live LinkedIn → board meter

Your LinkedIn post's reaction count, streamed onto the Arcade Coder in real time.

Setup (one time, ~1 min)

  1. Install the Tampermonkey extension in Chrome (free). A page script can't reach your board directly — LinkedIn blocks it — but Tampermonkey's privileged requests can.
  2. Click the Tampermonkey icon → Create a new script, delete the template, and paste the script below (use the copy button).
  3. Save it (File → Save or ⌘S).

Use it

  1. Open your post on its own page (click the post's timestamp so it's the only post on screen — that makes the count unambiguous).
  2. The board switches to the reaction meter and starts climbing within a few seconds. A tiny "▶ board" badge in the corner shows it's live; click it to pause.

The script only reads the number on a page you're already viewing and pushes it to 127.0.0.1 on your own Mac — nothing leaves your machine.
